Design system · verified

Nura Solid Surfaces

The wallet's surfaces were translucent white over three radial-gradient orbs, with a backdrop-filter on every card, row, button and input. They are opaque now. Both palettes below are the shipped token values, pinned side by side — because the question a de-glass has to answer is whether it holds in both themes at once.

The surface ramp

The old ramp was alpha: 25%, 60% and 40% white. Alpha has no colour of its own, so the muted control at 4% white in dark rendered as nothing once there was no blurred backdrop under it to tint. Separation now comes from a lightness step, a hairline, and a shadow that hints rather than carries.

Two failures the rewrite removed

Re-authoring the fills meant re-checking every pair that carries a label. Two that shipped were below the 4.5:1 floor, on the two most-pressed buttons in the app.

Reversed text on dark primary

3.49:1→5.70:1

The old fill was a brighter blue that looked better alone. The resting value is now whatever leaves the hover step room to lighten and still clear the floor.

Reversed text on dark danger

3.79:1→5.77:1

Same correction, same reason. Its hover step lands at 4.66:1, which is what caps how bright the resting fill can be.

Controls at rest, hover, press, focus and disabled

Focus is focus-visible now and one colour rather than five. Pinning a transparent outline at rest suppresses the browser's own ring, so the old focus: spelling flashed on mouse clicks while the bare variant — the nav tabs, every history row — showed nothing at all on Tab.

Rows, and one idea of “selected”

Four surfaces had each arrived at their own selected tint, and the nav bar used a pressed-state token as a resting fill. One treatment now, and it pairs a border with the wash so the state does not rest on colour alone.

The scales that had no tokens

@theme declared colour and font-size only, which is exactly where the drift was: nine radii, three unnamed shadow geometries, five durations across three languages, and three bare z-indexes with the highest claimed by two unrelated things.

Every pair the build checks

Body text clears 4.5:1 on every surface it can land on; a control boundary clears 3:1. script/contrast.js parses the stylesheet itself — not a copy kept in step by hand — and runs as part of npm run build, so the palette cannot regress quietly.
